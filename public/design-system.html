The Design System

The language of a quiet-luxury estate concierge — alabaster ground, warm ink, and one clay accent, used sparingly.

01
Foundations

Principles

Six tenets that decide every screen. When in doubt, subtract.

i
Quiet luxury
Restraint over decoration. Space is the material. Nothing shouts; everything is considered.
ii
Hairlines, not cards
Structure with thin rules and generous margins. Avoid boxes, shadows and containers where a line will do.
iii
One clay accent
A single warm terracotta, used sparingly — an active state, a rising sun, a figure that matters. Never as decoration.
iv
Architectural line-art
Illustration is single-weight ink drawing — elevations, thresholds, the estate itself. No fills, no gradients.
v
Unhurried motion
Movement is slow and eased, revealing content rather than performing. The dawn rises; it does not flash.
vi
A grid of four
All spacing steps in multiples of 4px. Type sits on a small, deliberate scale. Rhythm before ornament.
02
Brand

Identity

Alba is dawn. The mark is a sun rising into a doorway — a threshold met by first light.

The mark — doorway & rising sun
ALBA
Maison
Wordmark lockup — Fraunces, MAISON tracked to equal width

Construction. The mark is drawn in the same single-weight ink language as the estate elevations — a segmental arch on a threshold line, with a clay half-disc rising from it. It carries the app's one accent. In the lockup, MAISON is letter-spaced (~0.71em at this size) so its ink width matches ALBA exactly and both edges sit flush. On launch, the sun rises into the arch — the brand's single signature motion.

Spacing & grid

One scale, all multiples of four. Sections breathe at 40px; rows sit at 16px.

--sp-2 · 8px — tight pairs

--sp-6 · 32px — figures, feature spacing

06
Foundations

Motion

One easing curve throughout. Content rises into place; it never bounces or spins.

The ease
A single standard curve — a gentle out-ease used for every transition, reveal and toggle.
--e: cubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1)
Rise & stagger
Elements fade up 12px into place, cascading ~80ms apart, so a screen assembles top-to-bottom.
fadeup .7s · stagger .08s
The dawn
The signature moment — on launch the clay sun rises into the arch, then the screen fades to reveal the app.
splashSun 1s · scaleY 0→1
